# Turning customer records into a persona (scene 03)

Updated 2026-10-27 for G01-T06. Scene 03 uses Amberkloof, a made-up brand, and an illustrative sample of 60 orders. It makes no claim about a real business.

## The method on screen: Gather, Group, Describe, Test

1. **Gather.** Start with records you already have: orders, inquiries, repeat orders, where people found you. Add a short opt-in checkout survey (age group, sex, what it's for). Without records you are guessing. HubSpot's guidance starts here: look through your contacts database for trends in how customers find you, use what forms and analytics already collect, then talk to 3 to 5 good customers per persona.
2. **Group.** Sort the records by what people actually do (buy gifts, reorder for themselves, buy refill packs for a guesthouse), not by who you assumed they were. Data-driven persona research groups behaviour first, then attaches the demographic groupings that matter (An et al., 2018). NN/g's statistical personas use the same idea at scale, clustering survey answers, and give each persona a share of the base.
3. **Describe.** Write the persona from the biggest or most valuable group: age group, sex, area, circumstances and where they found you. Keep it to a handful of fields. NN/g warns that personas built only from demographics or analytics have limited use and must stay rooted in what customers actually said and did.
4. **Test.** A persona is a working guess. Check the next ten orders against it and update it when it stops matching. HubSpot calls personas "living documents" to refine as real customers behave differently. The 15-year review of data-driven personas (Salminen et al., 2021) flags evaluation as the weak spot, so testing is part of the method, not an extra.

## Race and POPIA

Race is special personal information under POPIA section 26. The Information Regulator's guidance note lists "race or ethnic origin" in that category. Scene 03 shows race only as a field marked "Only if they tell you", with the line: "Race is special personal information under POPIA. Record it only when customers choose to tell you, never guess it." Owner decision G3.

## AI and privacy

Use AI to summarise anonymised counts and draft questions. Never paste customer names or contact details into a public AI tool. A person checks the source records and the conclusion.
