## Before giving it away

- It's built on Postiz, which is open source (AGPL-3.0). Rebranding is allowed. If you host it for the 3 winners, give them a link to the source and keep a small "Built on Postiz" credit in the footer or the About page.
- The Agent and AI writing need an AI key (`OPENAI_API_KEY`) in the Docker settings. Without one, the AI buttons show but don't answer.
- Each social platform needs its own app keys before a winner can connect it.
