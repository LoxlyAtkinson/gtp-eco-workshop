# Design Loop bar: Oryzo and supplied motion references

Observed 2026-10-24 in an actual browser. Comparison target: [Oryzo](https://oryzo.ai/), with the user's [floating-panel](https://pin.it/9JJqfW1Yx) and [product-out-of-phone](https://pin.it/2vF0x5b22) references for specific motion. This file describes checkable mechanisms, not assets to reproduce.

1. **One object carries several chapters.** Oryzo's cork object persists through a cutting-board opening and into a dark hero while the environment, camera and text change. GTP's compass/customer/ad thread must similarly transform into the next idea, not disappear behind unrelated slides.
2. **The camera reveals meaning.** Oryzo starts close on construction geometry and reveals the finished object before broad product copy. GTP should reveal the ten orders before naming its ICP, then reveal the ad only after channel/voice choices.
3. **Spatial layers have different jobs.** Oryzo's 3D object sits in front of a physical surface and behind foreground marks/text. The new GTP product image must visibly overlap a device frame and cast a coherent shadow; controls and copy stay crisp in front.
4. **The visual scale changes by chapter.** Oryzo alternates extreme close product views, smaller contextual product views, diagrams and editorial text. GTP needs at least four distinct scene treatments (object reveal, sortable evidence, sideways channel/creative gallery, conversational path, measurement) rather than one repeating card entrance.
5. **Each effect resolves into a useful action.** Oryzo's interactions expose a product claim or choice. The supplied reels resolve floating planes or products into a CTA. GTP's receipt sorting should produce an ICP, its ad should resolve into an inquiry route, and its website should lead to a clear menu/quote choice.
6. **Contrast is local and deliberate.** Oryzo can let a dark object fill the centre because text occupies distinct edge zones. GTP must keep captions and teaching copy in dedicated readable zones, including mid-scroll, on a 1366×768 projector and 390×844 phone.
7. **The final frame explains the point of the spectacle.** Oryzo's ending states the studio proposition after the fictional product demonstration. GTP's ending must return the entire journey to one customer, channel, action and measure, then hold the QR/link long enough to use.

The craft critic should inspect Oryzo and rendered GTP frames side by side, judge these mechanisms only, and name the single largest visible gap. It must not infer quality from source or from this document alone.
