## Outcome and scope

South African small-business owners can follow a 60-minute ECO presentation on desktop/projector and phone, practise one decision at each stage, and proceed to a business-analysis tool. The public entry is the github.io root; the participant entry is `/tool/`. The organiser presents with keyboard/click controls; participants can self-review. Protected report/admin roles are specified in the goal contract.

Preserve the 15-scene sequence, ECO framework, 30/60/90 website plan, responsible AI guidance, Attract → Interact close, selected reference links, and separate report-delivery versus marketing choice. Original visuals and illustrative business data are permitted. Desktop, 1366×768 projector, and 390×844 phone are required. English first; no other locale requested. Respect keyboard and reduced motion.
