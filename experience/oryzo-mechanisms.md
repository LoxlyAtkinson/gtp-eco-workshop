# Oryzo reference teardown — observed 2026-10-23

Reference: https://oryzo.ai/ (the site identifies its product as fictional). These are interaction mechanisms to learn from, not assets to copy.

1. The opening uses a complete visual world: textured work surface, an object at the centre, foreground objects and large typography occupy separate planes. Scrolling moves the object and composition instead of just revealing more text.
2. A persistent product object carries continuity across scene cuts. Its scale and context change while the visitor keeps a clear focal point.
3. Navigation names a few chapters; the current chapter remains visible while scrolling through many beats. The visitor knows where they are even during transitions.
4. Typography changes scale drastically between a chapter opening and its supporting explanation. Short statements land before detail appears.
5. The site alternates warm lit scenes and dark theatrical scenes. Contrast marks a new act rather than decorating each repeated section.
6. Small active demonstrations (hover hand, message flip, product choices) make an assertion testable. Interaction gives feedback immediately and then returns to the story.
7. Scroll movement is paced like a timeline, with pinned moments, depth and object movement. A direct chapter link remains available for visitors who do not want to scrub through every beat.

GTP translation: an original ECO signal object persists across the 15 workshop beats; colour/scale and chapter titles change at Engage, Convert, Optimize and Attract/Interact. The teaching moments become live, local UI exercises. Presenter buttons and direct hash access remain for the 60-minute session. Motion uses CSS/WAAPI and has an instant reduced-motion path.
