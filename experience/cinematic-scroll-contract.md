# Cinematic scroll release contract

Active contract for the expanded 2026-10-24 presentation request. Progress: `experience/progress.md`; production storyboard and 15-scene map: `experience/cinematic-journey-plan.md`. The earlier `cinematic-contract.md` and its local reviews are historical and do not accept this revision. The current published github.io version remains the earlier accepted release.

## Outcome and boundary

In a 60-minute presentation for small-business owners, ordinary scrolling transforms one idea into the next across all 15 scenes. The presenter can pause at any moment without losing readable teaching content. The journey concretely demonstrates customer evidence → ICP → platform choice → original ads → brand voice → website plan → interaction → conversion → optimization. Amina's Kitchen is a labelled fictional case. Preserve the ECO identity, 15-scene curriculum, speaker notes, scene overview, keyboard navigation, QR, responsible AI guidance, 30/60/90 website plan and honest inactive-tool preview. The public root and `/tool/` remain separate from the unready protected service. Use original assets; do not copy external reference media or branding.

Supported entries: github.io root and direct section/beat hashes. Roles: organiser presenting and participants reviewing on their own. Required viewports: 1366×768 projector and 390×844 phone. English; keyboard, native touch scroll, reduced motion, motion pause and print. No additional locale requested. Do not publish participant records, service secrets or raw review evidence in the Pages artifact.

## Numbered acceptance criteria

C1 Opening introduces ECO with a clear workshop promise, original three-dimensional visual and a visible scroll invitation; the first scroll causes a meaningful scene change.

C2 All 15 scenes form one scroll-driven visual journey. Each scene has a planned start, mid-scroll transformation and handoff into the next, using continuing objects and varied camera/composition. Native scroll remains usable during a 60-minute talk; the deck does not jump a whole scene on one wheel gesture.

C3 The ICP scene visibly turns a ten-order evidence sample into a provisional buyer segment and explains that real business data must replace the example. Its reveal/inspection works by pointer, touch and keyboard.

C4 The ad scene shows at least three complete original specimen placements (feed, vertical storyboard, search/Business): actual visual, hook/headline, body or script, CTA and destination. Each is clearly illustrative, with no unverified performance claim. A 3D catering product crosses a device frame in the supplied reference's spatial spirit without copying its assets.

C5 The brand-voice scene compares a generic AI draft, explicit brand rules, a human edit and a finished caption; a user action visibly applies or resets the comparison. The channel and content lessons connect to the same fictional ICP.

C6 The 30/60/90 website plan includes a buyer phrase hypothesis, concrete service/FAQ/blog/page work, source/technical checks and measured inquiry outcomes. It does not claim actual ranking, citations or backlinks without evidence.

C7 Conversion and interaction scenes show a complete fictional ad/search → page/profile → CTA → inquiry → helpful reply → quote/follow-up route, with a functional local menu/quote demonstration. No real customer contact occurs.

C8 Optimization and Attract/Interact lessons show a decision from illustrative measures and demonstrate visual hierarchy, attraction and an obvious next action using the original ad case. Source inspiration is identified and linked but no external site is required to understand the lesson.

C9 Presenter controls, notes, overview, direct hashes, next/back, keyboard and touch work with the scroll model. Focus is visible, controls give feedback, and interactive clicks do not trigger accidental navigation. A paused presentation can resume at the same beat.

C10 Normal-motion scenes are alive through scroll-linked transformation, with restrained idle motion only where useful. A visible pause control, reduced-motion route, hidden-tab pause, media fallback and complete print output avoid forced motion, unreadable intermediate states and missing content.

C11 Every scene and ad remains readable/reachable at 1366×768 and 390×844; no horizontal overflow, hidden key content, obstructing fixed controls or unreadable contrast. Original generated assets include text alternatives and do not carry essential baked text.

C12 The tool handoff and `/tool/` remain clearly marked preview with disabled submission; the presentation collects no participant details or implies scanning/email/report delivery is live.

C13 The public github.io source matches the independently reviewed revision. Browser evidence includes starts and mid-scroll states across all 15 scenes on both required viewports, interactions, notes/overview, direct route recovery, pause, reduced motion, print, media fallback, close/QR and `/tool/`; record measured viewports and revision. An independent raw review, repair loop, judge-authored machine verdict and acceptance checker pass under `experience/experience-acceptance.md`.

## User-required blind review and scoring

At every review round, the builder and each of three fresh independent reviewers must explicitly answer: Does the whole presentation deliver a cinematic, scroll-evolving journey from start to finish, informed by Oryzo and the supplied animation/product references, with original 3D imagery, actual complete illustrative ad examples, and lived demonstrations of finding the ICP, finding a brand voice, engaging and interacting with customers? Does every page visibly evolve through scrolling while remaining readable when the presenter pauses? Record a score from 0 to 100 and the single biggest remaining gap. A score below 100 is FAIL; all three independent scores and the builder's score must reach 100 before release. Scores do not override the numbered criteria or acceptance gates. Reviewers receive the frozen contract and rendered/browser evidence, without implementation source, builder rationale or prior verdicts. Brief, system and craft reviewers retain their distinct primary lenses. Keep raw failures and commission fresh reviews after each repair; do not stop at a fixed round count. Disclose that site names and actual rendered branding may be visible even when provenance and prior work are blinded.
