# Cinematic presentation change contract

Active contract for the user's 2026-10-23 request: turn the published GTP ECO presentation into an interactive cinematic experience inspired by `https://oryzo.ai/`. Progress: `experience/progress.md`. Prior presentation-only acceptance remains historical evidence for commit `c31e608`; it does not accept this changed experience.

## Outcome and boundaries

Small-business owners should experience ECO as a story with visible progression, spatial depth, meaningful interaction and deliberate transitions, while the organiser can still deliver the 60-minute session from 15 scenes. Preserve the factual teaching content, GTP identity, source caveats, notes, scene overview, keyboard navigation, QR to the presentation and inactive participant-tool preview. Use original visual assets and interaction concepts; do not copy Oryzo's coaster, images, copy or source code. Entry is the github.io root; tool preview stays at `/tool/`. Support observed 1366×768 projector and 390×844 phone viewports, keyboard, touch/scroll, reduced motion and print. English only.

## Numbered criteria and coverage map

C1 The opening presents ECO as a layered cinematic world, with an original visual object, a clear first action and readable workshop promise.

C2 Moving between all 15 scenes produces deliberate changes in composition, colour, motion and chapter state, not the same card animation repeated. The sequence preserves Engage → Convert → Optimize → Attract/Interact → close.

C3 At least four scene interactions teach or demonstrate a decision (customer evidence, channel focus, AI voice/content, website plan, conversion or measurement). Each has visible feedback and a usable keyboard/touch route.

C4 Presenter controls, overview, notes, direct hashes and next/back remain reliable; wheel or swipe navigation does not steal scrolling from long phone scenes or focused controls.

C5 Motion is paced and purposeful, with a complete reduced-motion route, no flash or unreadable intermediate state, and useful static/print fallbacks.

C6 Every scene remains readable at projector and phone sizes, including its teaching example and next action; no content is permanently hidden behind controls or off-screen. Header, footer and QR remain usable.

C7 The tool handoff remains transparent that scanning is a preview with disabled submissions; the new experience must not collect participant information or imply the service is live.

C8 The public github.io revision matches the reviewed source and complete browser journey; capture representative opening, transitions, interactions, notes, closing, phone and reduced-motion states with measured viewport and source revision.

Use the shared experience acceptance method for independent review and repair. Maintain prior raw reports; the new final verdict must be scoped to this cinematic revision.
