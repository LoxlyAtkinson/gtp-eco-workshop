# Motion Video + GSAP audit — 15-scene ECO presentation

**Status:** implementation audit, 2026-10-27 05:01 SAST. Active release contract: `cinematic-scroll-contract.md`; progress: `progress.md`. This is a plan grounded in current local rendering, not a new acceptance verdict. The public github.io root still serves the older accepted deck.

## Brief and method

The presenter needs each numbered scene to begin moving on its own, progress slowly while speaking, and hold before the next numbered scene. Next begins the following scene; manual scroll, Pause scroll, notes, an open interaction, reduced motion and a hidden tab must have understandable priority. The audience needs to see a single fictional customer journey become evidence, an ad, a reply and a measured decision. Each paused frame must remain legible at 1366×768 and 390×844.

The installed Claude Video 2.0 motion skill supplies **one look, real reference mechanisms, a timed storyboard, one clock and frame inspection**. The general Video skill supplies media inspection and export QA when an MP4 is actually useful. GSAP Timeline and ScrollTrigger supply labelled, deterministic scroll-bound beats; GSAP Performance favours transforms and opacity on a few visible elements. Code-driven motion is the default because the presenter must pause at arbitrary beats and retain selectable DOM text and controls. A raster video is only an accent, never the sole teaching layer. No soundtrack or credit spend is needed for this audit.

## Production order and measurable gates

1. **Auto-scroll contract first.** Keep native scroll and the existing presenter stop before every next scene. Reduce the apparent 1.8-second opening delay only after an actual Next-arrival test; tune a floor for visible early motion, especially scene 08 on phone. A scene should show a meaningful change within the first spoken sentence, finish its internal beats inside its allocated minutes and never cross to the next scene without Next. Pause, manual input, notes, dialog, hidden tab and reduced motion must not restart at a different beat.
2. **Repair midpoint legibility before embellishment.** Handoffs 01, 04, 09 and 14 need one readable foreground artifact at a time. At the 50% frame, use an opaque carrier or a clean occlusion turn, not two semi-transparent cards. Recheck all 14 handoffs after repair.
3. **Build the causal chain in three passes:** 01–05 customer/placement, 06–10 message/sale/measure, 11–15 visual/interaction/plan/close. Each scene gets a one-clock GSAP timeline with named beats and scroll progress as its clock. Existing presenter buttons seek or hold those beats; no second timer competes with auto-scroll.
4. **Video QA at every scene:** storyboard start, 50%, end and both sides of each boundary; inspect real screenshots rather than source alone. Reserve MP4 export for media that adds something code motion cannot. The existing scene-13 draft is the only essential clip candidate. Any new rendered clip follows the motion-video skill's three-still approval before MP4 export.
5. **Release gates:** recheck all 15 scenes and 14 handoffs at projector and phone sizes, auto entry/hold/Next, every interaction, reduced motion, media failure, print and exact-source hashes. Preserve raw independent failures; obtain fresh builder and three independent blind 100/100 scores and public C1–C13 verdict before replacing the github.io release.
