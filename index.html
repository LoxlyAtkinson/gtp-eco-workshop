What the owner assumed

“Busy parents need dinner.”

What the last 10 orders suggest

Example only. Your own orders and inquiries should decide your first segment.
Use sales, inquiry, repeat purchase and location data if available. Ask audience to note one evidence source they already own. Demographics can help, but needs and buying context matter more.

ENGAGE · WHAT

Find the question
behind the scroll.

01

Read customer questions, reviews and inquiry messages. Note exact words people use.

02

Observe

Study competitors’ public posts, offers and comments. Treat engagement as a signal, not sales proof.

03

Make something useful

Answer a real question, demonstrate a result or explain a choice. Test the hook and call to action.

AMINA’S KITCHEN“How much food should I order for a 20-person meeting?”One question → a short video, a website FAQ and a quote prompt.
Show a simple competitor-analysis worksheet. Ask what successful posts have in common: hook, format, proof, offer or timing. Look for unanswered customer questions. Do not copy creative assets.
ENGAGE · CREATE WITH AI

AI drafts faster.
You make it yours.

GIVE AI THE BRIEF

“We make generous, reliable meeting lunches in Johannesburg. Our customers are office managers. We sound warm, direct and helpful. Never promise same-day delivery.”

GENERIC DRAFT

“Elevate your corporate catering experience with our premium culinary solutions!”

HUMAN EDIT

“Feeding 20 people at 12:30? Tell us the date and dietary needs. We’ll suggest a lunch that travels well.”

Give AI real examples of your voice, your offer and your limits. Check every claim before posting.
Demonstrate a prompt for ideas, captions, descriptions, simple visuals or short video scripts. Show two authentic writing samples, then edit the result. Never feed private customer records into a public prompt without authorization.
ENGAGE · WEBSITE PRACTICE

Your website needs
a 90-day rhythm.

01–30 DAYS

Find the gaps

Week 1: review customer questions and Search Console queries if available. Week 2: choose one buyer phrase. Week 3: improve one offer page. Week 4: publish one useful FAQ.

31–60 DAYS

Publish the answers

Weeks 5–6: publish two answers with real examples and sources. Week 7: link them to the offer page. Week 8: test the quote path on a phone.

61–90 DAYS

Measure and refresh

Weeks 9–12: compare qualified inquiries by page and source. Refresh the weak answer, ask for honest reviews after delivery, and set the next quarter’s topics.

AMINA’S KITCHEN · WORKED DECISION“Meeting lunches Johannesburg” → one service page → quote requests

Test this phrase against actual query data. Without it, the keyword is a hypothesis, not a ranking promise.

SEO helps pages be found; AEO makes answers useful; GEO checks a sample of AI answers. AI citations and backlinks require actual sources, not a guessed score.

Define SEO, AEO and GEO plainly. Explain that discoverable, useful, technically accessible pages are the foundation. Show how an AI-assisted 30/60/90 plan still needs real data, editing and review.

CONVERT · FOLLOW THROUGH

A sale is often won
after the first reply.

  1. 01Inquiry

    Record the customer’s need and permission to reply.

  2. 02Helpful answer

    Give a useful response quickly.

  3. 03Quote or next step

    Make the decision easy to understand.

  4. 04Agreed follow-up

    Follow up at a sensible time and honour preferences.

  5. 05Delivery and review

    After the work, invite an honest review.

A simple spreadsheet or CRM pipeline is enough to start: inquiry → quote → order → delivered → follow-up.
Explain funnels as sequences with a goal, not as endless automated messages. A welcome, abandoned cart or onboarding journey must fit customer expectations and permission.
