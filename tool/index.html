How your details are used

GTP uses your business inputs to prepare the scan and your name and email to send you the report. Supabase stores the report and contact choice; Firecrawl reads public website pages; the Smart AI Solutions mail server sends the email. If you add a public social profile, Apify may sample its public posts. GTP keeps stored scan and contact records for 30 days, then purges them. Marketing is sent only if you tick the box above. To ask about your data or request deletion, email loxly@smartaisolutions.co.za.
