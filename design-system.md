# GTP ECO presentation design system

Derived from the existing `styles.css`/`cinematic.css` and project content on 2026-10-24; no separate brand document was found. Preserve the current GTP mark and identity, not the fictional Amina brand as the workshop's owner.

- **Typography:** Manrope for large editorial headings and controls; DM Sans for body. Hero scale 4–9.8rem on desktop, 3.25–5.3rem on phone. Strong weight, tight heading tracking, comfortable body leading. No essential text baked into images.
- **Core palette:** night `#0d1714`, cream `#f3ead8`, mint `#91d8bd`, copper `#e5a46f`; paper `#f5f1e8`, ink `#171b1b`. Engage is green, Convert warm copper, Optimize blue, Attract green/cream. Background changes support meaning and must maintain readable contrast.
- **Composition:** editorial asymmetric spreads, generous negative space around a single focal object, max one clear primary question or action per beat. Meaningful media has a caption and readable text counterpart. Amina's Kitchen specimens use a cohesive deep-green/cream/copper subpalette but are always marked fictional.
- **Depth:** a far environment, one focal original object, a near frame/annotation and stable DOM type. Differential travel creates depth. Use contact/shadow and occlusion rather than unrelated floating ornaments.
- **Motion:** native scroll is the timeline; entering a new idea changes spatial relationship and understanding. No text should become unreadable mid-transition. Hover is optional enhancement. Pause and reduced-motion preserve every teaching point. Sound is off unless explicitly started.
- **Controls:** visible focus, persistent notes/overview/back/next, direct-route recovery, large touch targets. The presentation must be usable while the presenter stops mid-beat.
- **Truthfulness:** generated food and ads are original illustrative specimens. Never imply Amina's Kitchen, placements, search rank, testimonials or results are real. The participant scan remains a preview until its protected service is verified.
