HOW TO USE YOUR SKILLS (the way we did it on stage)

Start here: open claude.ai on a computer and upload one skill. Takes 3 minutes.


PART 1. PUT THE SKILLS INTO CLAUDE (once, about 3 minutes per skill)

1. Open claude.ai and sign in. The free plan works.
2. Go to Settings > Capabilities. Switch on "Code execution and file creation"
   (newer layout: "Create and edit files").
3. Go to Customize > Skills. Click +, then "Create skill", then "Upload a skill".
4. Open the folder upload-to-claude in your zip. Upload ONE zip, for example
   campaign-plan.zip. Don't unzip it. One zip is one skill. Repeat for the others.
5. Check the switch next to each skill is on.

Using Claude Code? Copy the skill folders into ~/.claude/skills/ instead.


PART 2. RUN THE STAGE DEMO ON YOUR OWN BUSINESS

Do these in ONE chat, in this order. Each step uses what the step before found.
Swap the words in [brackets] for your own.

Step 1. Know your website
"Use the seo-audit skill on [yourwebsite.co.za]. Tell me what I sell, who it's
for, and the 5 things to fix first so people in South Africa find me on Google."

Step 2. Know your competitors
"Use the competitive-brief skill. Find the businesses in South Africa that sell
what I sell to the same people. For each one: what they say, what they charge if
it's on their site, and one thing they do that I don't."

Step 3. Know what people are saying (needs the last30days setup in its SKILL.md)
"Use the last30days skill on [your product or industry]. What are people asking
about this month?" It reads YouTube and Reddit, plus X.

Step 4. Make a 30-day plan
"Use the campaign-plan skill. Using my website, my competitors and what people
are saying, make a 30-day plan. 5 steps. One platform. One number to check
every Friday."

Step 5. Get ad ideas
"Use the ad-creative skill. Give me 5 ad ideas for [one product]. Each one
needs a hook, the words on the ad, and why it beats the competitors above."

Step 6. Make it sound like you
"Use the slopmonster skill on idea number [2]. Take out anything that sounds
like AI wrote it. Keep the facts."

Step 7. Write the ad and the follow-up
"Use the draft-content skill to write the final ad for Instagram. Then use the
email-sequence skill to write 3 follow-up emails for people who reply."


PART 3. KEEP GOING (15 minutes, every weekday)

- Monday: brand-review. Paste your post and ask "Does this sound like my brand?"
- Wednesday: content-creation. "Turn this week's best post into a WhatsApp
  message and a Facebook post."
- Friday: performance-report. Paste your numbers and ask "What worked, and what
  do I do next week?"


TIPS

- Name the skill in your message: "Use the [skill name] skill to..."
- Give it facts: your website, your prices, who buys from you.
- Read every draft before it goes out. Your edit is what makes it yours.
- Claude says a skill won't upload? Attach its SKILL.md file to a normal chat
  and say "Follow these instructions."
