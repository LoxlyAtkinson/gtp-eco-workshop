# Smart AI Funnels

A funnel is the list of things that happen after a person shows interest. Each step has one job, one trigger and one message. This skill builds two:

- **Sales funnel:** inquiry → first reply → offer → order → thank you → review → next order.
- **Support funnel:** question → fast answer → fix or hand over to a person → check it worked → review.

## 1. Ask first (one message, all at once)

1. What do you sell, and what does one order cost on average?
2. Where do people first reach you? (WhatsApp, Instagram DM, website form, phone, walk-in)
3. What do they ask most before they buy? List the top 3.
4. What do they ask most after they buy? List the top 3.
5. Which tools do you use now? (WhatsApp Business app, an email tool, a spreadsheet, a CRM, nothing)
6. B2C or B2B? How long from first question to paying, in days?

If the person gave a website, read it first and fill in what you can. Ask only for what is missing.

## 2. Map the funnel

Write a table for each funnel:

| Step | Trigger | Wait | Channel | The one job of this message | What counts as done |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Rules:
- One message, one job. Never sell and ask for a review in the same message.
- The first reply goes out within 5 minutes during working hours. Say who you are in the first line.
- Every step has a way out: a person can reply STOP or "talk to a person" at any point.
- Support beats sales. If a support question arrives mid-sales-funnel, pause the sales steps.
- Keep the sales funnel to 4 to 6 messages over 2 to 3 weeks. After that, stop.

## 3. Write every message

For each step give: the channel, the exact text, the button or link, and when it sends.

WhatsApp rules (Meta, checked for South Africa):
- You may reply freely for 24 hours after the customer's last message. Outside that window you need an approved template.
- Templates are Marketing, Utility or Authentication. Order updates and appointment reminders are Utility. Offers are Marketing.
- Ask before you message first: the customer must opt in. Keep proof of the opt-in.
- Short. Write like a person texting, not a brochure. One emoji at most.

Email rules:
- Subject under 45 characters. One link. A plain-text version with no formatting.
- Unsubscribe link in every marketing email.

South Africa:
- POPIA: only message people who agreed to it, say why you have their number, and honour "stop" at once.
- Prices in rand, dates as 3 March, times in 24-hour or "3pm".

## 4. Track it

Name one number per funnel to check every Friday:
- Sales funnel: inquiries that became orders.
- Support funnel: questions answered within one hour.

## Output

1. The two funnel tables.
2. Every message, in order, ready to paste.
3. The Friday numbers.
4. What to set up in their current tools to send it (WhatsApp Business labels and quick replies, or their email tool's automation).
