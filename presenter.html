How to start
  1. Press Win+P. Pick Extend.
  2. Press Open audience screen.
  3. Drag it to the projector. Click its title bar. Press F11.
  4. Click this page once. Press Mic on.
  5. Say the Next cue word, or press Next.

Keys: Right or Page Down = Next. Left or Page Up = Back. M = mic. Esc = hold.
