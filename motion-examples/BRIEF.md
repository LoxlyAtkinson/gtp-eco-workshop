# Amina motion studies — presentation examples

Active experience contract: `../experience/cinematic-scroll-contract.md`. Progress: `../experience/progress.md`.

## Source and intent

These are original, fictional motion examples for the GTP workshop. They use the editorial-paper style's grid, ink rules, restrained typography and one accent, recoloured to the presentation's warm paper and deep green. The method follows the [Claude Video 2.0 skill](https://github.com/ItsssssJack/claude-video-2): specific brief, one motion clock per piece, inspect real frames, and revise before final delivery. The supplied [video walkthrough](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KphrV76DJoI) demonstrates that process and the distinction between code graphics, owned media and generated clips. The original Amina product illustration is from this project; no third-party video or logo is copied. No generated clips or paid credits are used.

## Motion brief

Three 16:9, silent, 8–10 second studies share one fictional business and one accent colour (#B97042). Source copy remains visible in HTML when motion is unavailable. GSAP drives each study with a single paused timeline. ScrollTrigger starts the visible study once; presenter controls replay, pause and resume it. All movement uses transforms or opacity. No random timing. Reduced-motion viewers see completed, readable frames.

| Study | Visual decision | Exact teaching point | Final action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 / Who | Ten order slips sort into six meeting lunches and four family trays | Order type and group size are observed; buyer identity is still unknown | Ask the next inquirer who is arranging lunch |
| 02 / What | Original product crosses a social frame as the useful offer resolves | Hook, portion guidance, CTA and destination form one complete ad | Ask for a quote with date, guests and dietary needs |
| 03 / Then | The same question moves from inquiry to helpful reply and quote | Attention becomes a sale only when the next step is clear and followed through | Confirm a suitable option and agreed follow-up |

## Acceptance for these studies

Inspect opening, middle and final rendered browser frames at 1366×768 and 390×844. Verify readable labels, truthful illustrative claims, consistent Amina object and offer, replay/pause, keyboard, reduced motion and no horizontal overflow. They are supporting examples, not replacement proof for the full 15-scene presentation or public C13.
