# GTP ECO scroll experience brief

Creative authority: the owner supplied the subject, 60-minute duration, framework, draft presentation, named visual bar and references, and said to generate original images and use judgment. Authored decisions below are labelled. This is an existing presentation redesign, not a new brand launch.

1. **Vibe/references (user supplied):** “interactive cinematic experience like the oryzo.ai site”; scroll should evolve into the next section, product illustration like `https://pin.it/2vF0x5b22`. Earlier references: House Down Studios, Refero Styles, an Instagram reel and two Pinterest clips. Authored words: tactile, editorial, confident, useful.
2. **Journey (user supplied):** Engage (who, where, how; customer evidence, competitors and content), Convert (attention to inquiry to sale), Optimize (measure and double down), then Attract and Interact; 30/60/90 website plan and responsible AI; close with a participant tool handoff. Fifteen existing scenes carry this sequence.
3. **Energy curve (authored from the supplied teaching sequence):** curiosity → doubt → recognition → focus → appetite (ad reveal) → confidence (voice) → structure (website plan) → urgency (conversion) → reassurance (reply) → clarity (measurement) → admiration (attract) → control (interact) → discernment (AI taste) → anticipation (tool) → resolve (four commitments). Adjacent scenes must change the emotional job as well as visual treatment.
4. **Peak (authored):** “The ten real-looking order cards reorganised into one buyer, and the lunch product burst out of that buyer's phone into an ad I could actually use.” The largest scroll span belongs to the evidence-to-ad turn across scenes 03–05. No false business performance is implied.
5. **Signature move (authored):** the same evidence thread physically changes form across the whole ECO loop: receipt edges become ICP lines, channel lines become ad frames, the CTA line becomes a chat thread, and the thread bends into a metric curve. A user can stop and inspect each form.
6. **Aesthetic range (authored within existing identity):** cinematic editorial, not generic premium minimalism. Deep green/cream/copper, clear large typography, tactile 3D food and restrained glass planes. High variety across 15 teaching scenes.
7. **Structure (user supplied):** one start-to-finish journey through scrolling, with 15 navigable sections and presenter controls. Authored grammar: *guided field study*, a custom grammar because a teaching deck needs navigable checkpoints while the visual thread stays continuous. It forbids isolated slide wipes, a single passive video and endless identical pinned cards.
8. **Existing assets (observed):** 15-scene HTML/CSS/JS deck, GTP mark, original ECO compass PNG, QR, tool preview, speaker notes and presenter controls. Original transparent Amina lunch PNG/WebP was generated for the new illustration style. No existing brand system file; `design-system.md` has now been derived from the current source.

**What the attendee should believe by the end (authored synthesis):** one evidence-based customer, a suitable channel, a useful ad and a clear response path can be improved through a simple weekly measure.

**Next action:** save the presentation and write one customer, one channel, one action and one measure; the analysis tool remains a preview until the protected service is live.

**Tell-someone sentence:** “It's the workshop where my customers' orders turned into an actual ad, then into a conversation and a measurable sale as I scrolled.”

**Authored silence:** the presenter may hold each beat indefinitely for discussion. Idle movement is subtle and pausable; no information depends on it.
