# GTP ECO — 60-minute presenter guide

Audience: small-business owners. Example: **Amina’s Kitchen**, a fictional Johannesburg catering business. Keep the example clearly illustrative; no sales or demographic figures are real research. The on-screen deck is the audience view. Each scene also has a speaker note toggle.

| Scene | Minutes | Facilitation move | Participant output |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| 1. Opening | 2 | Ask where marketing time is being lost. | One frustration |
| 2. ECO map | 3 | Explain Engage → Convert → Optimize as a repeatable loop. | One business goal |
| 3. Who | 5 | Contrast the owner’s assumption with last orders/inquiries. | Customer hypothesis plus evidence source |
| 4. Where | 5 | Score Google/Maps, Facebook, Instagram/TikTok and WhatsApp by customer intent, effort and response capacity. | One discovery channel, one inquiry route |
| 5. What | 5 | Turn a real question into a useful post/page. Examine public competitor signals and gaps without copying. | One content question |
| 6. AI with a voice | 5 | Show brief → generic draft → human edit. Invite a rewrite. | One edited caption |
| 7. Website rhythm | 6 | Explain SEO, AEO and GEO, then map 30/60/90 days. | One page or search priority |
| 8. Conversion path | 5 | Trace source → destination → CTA → reply. | One clear CTA |
| 9. Follow-up | 4 | Walk an inquiry through answer, quote, agreed follow-up, delivery and review. | One missing stage |
| 10. Optimize | 4 | Separate impressions from qualified inquiries, quotes and sales. | One weekly measure |
| 11. Attract | 3 | Show Oryzo and House Down as visual references; ask what is noticed first. | One attention principle |
| 12. Interact | 3 | Show an original mock page; ask what the visitor can do next. | One interaction improvement |

| 15. Close | 3 | Ask for one customer, channel, action and measure for the next seven days. | Seven-day commitment |

Total: **60 minutes**. Use the scene overview to jump and the arrow keys to move. If behind schedule, shorten the technical detail in scene 7 before removing an audience exercise.

## Hands-on exercise: customer evidence

Ask owners to review their last ten orders or inquiries. Record: customer type, need, location, discovery source, quoted amount if relevant, outcome, and repeat purchase. Count the patterns. Do not upload identifiable customer records to an AI service. If the owner has little data, label the segment as a hypothesis and plan three customer conversations.

## Channel decision

Give each candidate channel a simple 1–3 score for (1) customer presence, (2) buying intent, (3) content effort they can sustain, and (4) ability to respond. Discuss the trade-off; the score is a decision aid, not a universal platform ranking. Choose one primary discovery channel and one inquiry route for a 30-day test.

## AI demonstration prompts

1. **Customer hypothesis:** “Using only these anonymized counts, identify patterns, alternative explanations and what data is missing. Do not invent demographics.”
2. **Content:** “Our customer asks [question]. Suggest five ways to answer it: short video, carousel, FAQ, product description and WhatsApp reply. Each must have one next action.”
3. **Voice:** “Our brand is [three traits]. Here are two genuine examples we wrote: [examples]. Rewrite this draft to match the examples. List claims I must verify.”
4. **30/60/90:** “Use the observed page and query data below. Propose priorities at 30, 60 and 90 days. Mark ideas without source data as hypotheses and name the measure for each action.”

Keep the human edit visible. Check prices, availability, service areas, testimonials, product claims and visual rights before publication.

## Website practice to demonstrate

- **SEO:** make useful pages accessible and understandable to search engines and people. Review titles, main headings, internal links, speed, mobile layout, local facts and clear next actions.
- **AEO:** make pages answer real questions directly with context and evidence. It is a content practice, not a separate guaranteed ranking mechanism.
- **GEO/AI visibility:** check whether a sample of relevant AI answer prompts names or cites the business, recording engine, prompt, date, location and actual citation. A sample cannot establish comprehensive visibility.
- Start keyword work with [Search Console queries and pages](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/10268906?hl=en) when the owner has access. Check technical discoverability and sitemaps with [Search Console](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/monitor-debug/search-console-start?hl=en), mobile/desktop experience with [PageSpeed Insights](https://developers.google.com/speed/docs/insights/v5/about), and relevant structured data with the [Rich Results Test](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data). A third-party keyword/backlink index is needed before reporting search volume or backlink coverage.
- In days 1–30, audit and prioritize one service page, one location/service phrase, FAQs, Google Business Profile details and a conversion path. In days 31–60, improve the service landing page and publish two evidence-based answers. In days 61–90, review actual inquiries and search data, update the weak pages and plan the next cycle.

## Examples and contingency

- [Oryzo](https://oryzo.ai/) is fictional and satirical. Discuss its striking idea and hierarchy, never repeat its product claims as facts.
- [House Down Studio](https://housedownstudios.co.za/) is a live portfolio example. Discuss proof and route to quote/WhatsApp. If unavailable, use the deck’s written analysis and original mock.
- [Refero styles](https://styles.refero.design/) is a reference library. Extract principles; do not copy assets.
- The “Last 30 Days” trend skill can supply current public conversation themes when available. Show the source and date, and treat social discussion as a signal rather than a proven customer need.
- If the participant API is not released, say so clearly. Use a labelled sample ECO report and invite participants to return to the link after verification. Do not demonstrate a mock as a live scan.
